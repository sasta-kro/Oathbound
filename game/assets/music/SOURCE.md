# Music sources

All tracks below are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) on
OpenGameArt.org. No attribution is legally required; the authors are credited
here anyway. Files are the authors' MP3s, renamed; no edits.

| File | Music id | Title | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `title_theme.mp3` | `title` | Fantasy Orchestral Theme | Joth | https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-orchestral-theme |
| `town_theme.mp3` | `town` | Town Theme RPG | cynicmusic (cynicmusic.com, pixelsphere.org) | https://opengameart.org/content/town-theme-rpg |
| `field_theme.mp3` | `field` | The Field Of Dreams | pauliuw | https://opengameart.org/content/the-field-of-dreams |
| `battle_theme.mp3` | `battle` | Battle Theme A | cynicmusic (cynicmusic.com, pixelsphere.org) | https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a |

Music ids are registered in `scripts/audio/music_service.gd`. Areas pick
theirs through `WorldArea.music_id`.
