# Black Knight_B (kingsworn)

Source: `assets/sprites/monster/Black Knight_B`, copied here because Godot can only
import files under the project root. Pack name and licence are not recorded in
the drop; fill them in before release.

Sheets are horizontal strips of 100x100 frames, no shadow variant. The art faces right.
Generated by `tools/import_monster_sprites.py`.
Recoloured to cold blue steel with blue-white visor fire, horns drawn on
the helm, a flame plume above it and a flickering fire rim around the
silhouette, by the importer. The fire burns out over the death strip.
